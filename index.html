CSCI Lead — Air Force Battlefield Airborne Communications Node (BACN)
Led the software (CSCI) development effort for the BACN program, an airborne communications relay system that extends tactical communication range and enables interoperability between otherwise incompatible radio and data networks.

BACN enables seamless exchange of voice, video, imagery, and digital messages across heterogeneous systems, allowing end users—such as ground Special Forces and airborne platforms—to communicate in real time across different technologies (e.g., a soldier using a commercial mobile device communicating with a fighter aircraft cockpit).

The system supports multiple tactical and legacy waveforms, including SINCGARS, DAMA, EPLRS, SADL, Link 16, TCDL, and 802.11b, acting as a critical interoperability bridge in contested environments.
